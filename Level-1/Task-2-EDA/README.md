# Stock Prices Data Visualization

## Objective

To create visual representations of stock market data and identify trends and relationships between variables.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## Visualizations Created

1. Line Chart – AAPL Closing Price Trend
2. Bar Chart – Top 5 Stocks by Average Closing Price
3. Scatter Plot – Open Price vs Close Price

## Key Insights

* Stock prices show fluctuations over time.
* Certain stocks have significantly higher average closing prices.
* Open and close prices exhibit a strong positive relationship.

## Conclusion

Data visualization helps understand stock performance and uncover patterns that may not be obvious from raw data alone.
